Executive Briefing
Revenue growth remains strong, led by high-performing enterprise hardware and top-tier B2B sales[cite: 1]. However, overall enterprise profitability is severely dragged down by heavy category discounting (Tables, Bookcases) and regional loss leaders in the South[cite: 1].

Key Business Insights

1. Uncontrolled Discounting
Insight: Deep discount rates exceeding 20% on high-volume sub-categories—specifically Tables and Bookcases—generate top-line revenue while consistently yielding negative net margins[cite: 1].

Recommendation: Enforce a strict 15% discount cap on low-margin furniture items and re-evaluate bundled promotional rules[cite: 1].
2. B2B Profit Stability
Insight: The Corporate and Small Business customer segments generate over 60% of overall profit due to larger order values and consistent reorder cycles compared to individual consumer purchases[cite: 1].

Recommendation: Reallocate sales incentives toward securing multi-year corporate contracts and offering tiered volume incentives rather than upfront price cuts[cite: 1].
3. Freight Costs on Low Basket Size
Insight: Express Air and Same Day shipping modes are frequently selected for low-margin Office Supplies orders, resulting in unrecovered freight costs that erase transaction profits[cite: 1].

Recommendation: Institute minimum basket thresholds ($50+) for expedited shipping eligibility or adjust shipping surcharge tiers for non-corporate accounts[cite: 1].
